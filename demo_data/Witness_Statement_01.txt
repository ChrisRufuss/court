STATEMENT OF WITNESS UNDER SECTION 180 BNS / 161 CR.P.C.

Witness Name: Vikram Shah
Age: 42 Years | Occupation: Tea Stall Vendor
Location of Stall: Opp. Palm Beach Road Junction, Sector 14

Statement Recorded on: 11 July 2026 by SI R. Patil

Content of Statement:
"I operate a roadside tea stall at Sector 14 junction. On the night of 10 July 2026, around 11:15 PM, I heard a loud screech of tyres followed by a heavy crash sound. I looked towards the junction and saw a black SUV hit a scooter driver. 

The SUV was traveling at very high speed and failed to apply brakes before impact. The scooter driver fell down bleeding from his leg. I saw the driver of the SUV briefly slow down under the street light before accelerating away. I recognized the driver as Rajesh K., who occasionally visits the nearby commercial complex. 

I immediately ran to help the injured person, gave him water, and called the emergency 112 helpline. The ambulance arrived about 20 minutes later."

Witness Signature / Thumb Impression: Vikram Shah
Investigating Officer: Sub-Inspector R. Patil
